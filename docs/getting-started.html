Guide

Getting started

Open hosepipe.freestar.network in your browser. Desktop Chrome or Edge is recommended; Firefox works well too.

Listen to a talkgroup

From a card — click Listen on any talkgroup in the grid.

Direct tune — enter a TG number under Listen to a talkgroup now and click Listen now. This works even when the talkgroup isn’t showing as active.

Shareable link — open a link like https://hosepipe.freestar.network/?tg=2350 to start already tuned.

Favorites — tap the star on a card to save it in your browser. Use the Favorites filter for quick retune.

Stop — click Stop on the card, in the listening desk, or in the mobile listening bar.

Find talkgroups

  • Filter talkgroups — search by TG number, alias, callsign, or radio ID
  • Country — narrow the grid by country; cards show flags when known
  • Favorites — show only talkgroups you have starred

Quiet standby talkgroups are hidden by default; use Show all standby if you need them.

Listening desk

The sidebar shows what you’re tuned to and whether audio is live.

  • Volume — slider and mute; your preference is remembered
  • Speech clarity — optional clearer voice; fine-tune on desktop, On/Off on mobile
  • Talkgroup details — expandable source information

On mobile, a listening bar at the bottom gives volume, clarity, and stop while tuned.

Activity trail & Net Control

Recently heard traffic appears in the sidebar. Collapse panels to save space.

Optional Net Control tools help with check-in order during a net — add stations, advance the list, keep notes, and copy the roster. Everything stays on your device.

Connection status

  • Connected — monitor link is healthy
  • Connecting / Reconnecting — restoring the link; tap to retry if stuck
  • Disconnected — link is down; tap to retry
  • ON AIR / READY — listening status for live or idle tuned audio

Reporting problems

Note the version in the page footer, your browser, the talkgroup ID, and whether speech clarity was on — then open an issue.
