Release candidate

0.7.3-rc.2

HosePipe 0.7.3-rc.2 is live at hosepipe.freestar.network. It is pre-stable — expect occasional rough edges before the stable release.

What works well

  • Live talkgroup monitoring and selective listening in the browser
  • Favorites, country filter, search, and recent activity
  • Shareable listen links that open already tuned
  • Optional Net Control for check-in order during a net
  • Collapsible sidebar panels, including rearrange / undock on desktop
  • Volume and mute remembered between visits
  • Speech clarity on desktop; Clarity On/Off on mobile
  • Desktop Chrome, Edge, and Firefox

Known limitations

  • Brief noise between transmissions on some talkgroups — try Speech clarity
  • Mobile background audio is not guaranteed
  • Listener counts reflect HosePipe sessions on that instance
  • Speech clarity fine-tuning is desktop-only
  • Net Control stays on your device only
